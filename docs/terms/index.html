Zach Prod — Terms of Service

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Zach Prod is a local desktop application that helps creators generate, review, and manually publish video content to accounts they own or are authorized to manage.

Use of TikTok

Users connect their own TikTok account and explicitly choose each video they send. Users remain responsible for their content, captions, rights, and compliance with TikTok’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

No automatic public posting

Zach Prod does not publish content to TikTok without a user action. Where available, the user selects the requested visibility before sending a video.
